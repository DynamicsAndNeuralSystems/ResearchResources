# Pathways to doing a PhD in the Dynamics and Neural Systems Group

### Doing a PhD with me at Sydney Uni

General information about doing a PhD at The University of Sydney is [here](https://sydney.edu.au/courses/courses/pr/doctor-of-philosophy-science.html) and the application process for graduate research is described [here](https://www.sydney.edu.au/study/applying/how-to-apply/postgraduate-research.html). The fundamental requirement to be admitted to a PhD is an academic qualification that included a significant research component. Winning a PhD scholarship is very competitive, especially for international students. Options are summarized [here](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/australian-government-research-training-program.html). If you're unsure about your competitiveness, feel free to contact me and I can help you to navigate the application process.

For the RTP scholarship, make sure you learn about it and apply by the deadlines listed [here](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/australian-government-research-training-program/rtp-international.html).

I have funding available to provide additional support to talented PhD students who are able to obtain competitive scholarship funding.

#### Full list of scholarships

A full list of research scholarship funding for the Faculty of Science at Sydney University is [here](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/domestic/postgraduate-research/faculty/science.html).

#### Funding for Local (Australian) PhD Students

You would typically apply for a Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship.

#### Funding for International PhD Students

* **International RTP Scholarship**. International Scholarships for Australia (Research Training Program or University of Sydney International Scholarship) are especially competitive. High marks are a prerequisite for obtaining PhD funding; only applicants with a weighted average mark (WAM) in excess of 80 (corresponding to a first-class honours) are considered, and typically the threshold is far, far higher than this minimum (90+ as minimum competitive level). As well as high marks, students typically require some research experience (such as a published paper), and students from highly-ranked institutions have an additional advantage.
  * Scholarships for international postgraduate research students are listed [here](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/international/postgraduate-research.html). Australia Awards are available for students from countries with which Australia has a bilateral aid program: [list of countries](https://dfat.gov.au/people-to-people/australia-awards/Pages/participating-countries.aspx).
* **CSC Scholarships**. There is also PhD funding for [Chinese nationals](https://sydney.edu.au/scholarships/e/china-scholarship-council-research-programs-scholarship.html) via the Chinese Scholarship Council (CSC).
* **ARC Scholarships**. Another route into a PhD is a scholarship attached to an Australian Research Council (ARC)-funded project. These are funded by individual research groups, typically to work on a pre-defined project, and are listed [here](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/d/arc-postgraduate-research-scholarship.html).

### Preparing your application

Details for admission into a PhD program at Sydney University are [here](https://sydney.edu.au/courses/courses/pr/doctor-of-philosophy-science.html). Your PhD application will need to include [a research proposal](https://www.sydney.edu.au/study/help/advice/how-to-write-a-research-proposal.html), which I will help you write around a specific research project, as well as two academic references.
