# Pathways to doing a PhD in the Dynamics and Neural Systems Group

### Doing a PhD with me at Sydney Uni

General information about doing a PhD at The University of Sydney is [here](https://sydney.edu.au/courses/courses/pr/doctor-of-philosophy-science.html) and the application process for graduate research is described [here](https://sydney.edu.au/study/admissions/apply/how-to-apply.html#uniqueId_nPUe33Za_5_button). The fundamental requirement to be admitted to a PhD is an academic qualification that included a significant research component. Winning a PhD scholarship is very competitive, especially for international students. Options are summarized [here](http://sydney.edu.au/scholarships/research/research-training-program.shtml). If you're unsure about your competitiveness, feel free to contact me and I can help you to navigate the application process.

For the RTP scholarship, make sure learn about it and apply by the deadlines listed [here](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/australian-government-research-training-program/rtp-international.html).

I have funding available to provide additional support to talented PhD students who are able to obtain competitive scholarship funding.

#### Full list of scholarships

A full list of research scholarship funding for the Faculty of Science at Sydney University is [here](http://sydney.edu.au/scholarships/research/faculty/science.shtml).

#### Funding for Local (Australian) PhD Students

You would typically apply for a Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship.

#### Funding for International PhD Students

* **International RTP Scholarship**. International Scholarships for Australia (Research Training Program or University of Sydney International Scholarship) are especially competitive. High marks are a prerequisite for obtaining PhD funding; only applicants with a weighted average mean in excess of 80 (corresponding to a first-class honours) are considered, and typically the threshold is far, far higher than this minimum (90+ as minimum competitive level). As well as high marks, students typically require some research experience (such as a published paper), and students from highly-ranked institutions have an additional advantage.
  * Some details of the criteria used are [here](http://sydney.edu.au/scholarships/docsschols/Higher_Degree_by_Research_Scholarship_Selection_Process.pdf). Some additional examples of funding sources for postgraduate research study are [here](http://sydney.edu.au/scholarships/postgraduate/international-postgraduate-scholarships.shtml). Australia Awards are available for students from countries with which Australia has a bilateral aid program: [list of countries](https://dfat.gov.au/people-to-people/australia-awards/Pages/participating-countries.aspx).
* **CSC Scholarships**. There is also PhD funding for [Chinese nationals](https://sydney.edu.au/scholarships/e/china-scholarship-council-research-programs-scholarship.html) via the Chinese Scholarship Council (CSC).
* **ARC Scholarships**. Alternative sources to joining the Science Faculty for a PhD are via the list of Australian Research Council-funded projects (which are funded by individual research groups to typically work on a pre-defined project) are listed here: [https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/d/arc-postgraduate-research-scholarship.html](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/d/arc-postgraduate-research-scholarship.html)

### Preparing your application

Details for admission into a PhD program at Sydney University are [here](https://sydney.edu.au/courses/courses/pr/doctor-of-philosophy-science.html)). Your PhD application will need to include [a research proposal](https://sydney.edu.au/campus-life/student-news/2017/05/30/how-to-write-a-research-proposal-for-a-strong-phd-application.html), which I will help you write around a specific research project, as well as two academic references.
